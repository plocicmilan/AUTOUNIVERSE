### PM2 debug i fix
**Problemi:**
- Stari PM2 daemon (pid 30856, root-owned) držao portove 3000/3002/4000
- `autopijaca` imala `MainThread` na 3001 — stari orphan process
- `instances: 1` bez `exec_mode` → PM2 defaultuje na `cluster` mode → EADDRINUSE pri restartu
- `/var/log/pm2/*.log` u vlasništvu root → EACCES
- `/var/www/autouniverse/aucore/data/` u vlasništvu root → readonly database greška
- `pm2-milan.service` koristio `Type=forking` + `PIDFile` — PM2 v7 ne piše pm2.pid

**Rešenja:**
- `sudo kill -9 30856 35042` — ubio stare procese
- `exec_mode: 'fork'` dodato u ecosystem.config.js (lokalno + server)
- `sudo chown -R milan:milan /var/log/pm2/ /var/www/autouniverse/`
- `sudo chmod 664 /var/log/pm2/*.log`
- `pm2-milan.service`: `Type=oneshot` + `RemainAfterExit=yes` (bez PIDFile)
